◆ id_device_feature_mask_e

enum id_device_feature_mask_e

Hardware feature masks.

A bit 1 means that the corresponding hardware feature is NOT present on the device.

Enumerator
DEVICE_FEATURE_NO_TOUCH_PANEL 

Device does not have a touch panel and does not support touch screen operation.

DEVICE_FEATURE_NO_HW_KEYBOARD 

Device does not have a hardware keyboard and/or prefer to use a software keyboard.

DEVICE_FEATURE_NO_FKEY 

Device does not have physical F-keys and alternative keys/menus must be used in place of them.
